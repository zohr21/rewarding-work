## How it works

Some tasks give you nothing enjoyable now (exercise, chores, admin). Some pleasures give you nothing useful later (a binge-worthy show, a favourite podcast). Temptation bundling pairs them: you only get the pleasure *while* you're doing the task.

Two things make it work. The task becomes more pleasant in the moment, and the treat becomes something you look forward to — which pulls you towards starting the task.

The strict rule matters: if the podcast is available any time, it doesn't pull you anywhere.

## Try it now

1. Write down one task you avoid that doesn't need your full attention (ironing, the commute, washing up, walking).
2. Write down one small pleasure you'd happily look forward to (a podcast series, a particular playlist, a special coffee).
3. Make the rule: *I only get [pleasure] while doing [task].*
4. Try it at least three times before judging it.
