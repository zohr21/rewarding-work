## How it works

Instead of asking "how long will this take?", you decide "how much time is this worth?" — then give it exactly that. When the box ends, you stop and move on, even if the result isn't perfect.

This helps in two directions. For tasks you avoid, a small box ("just 15 minutes on the report") makes starting less daunting. For tasks that expand forever (polishing slides, researching), the box stops you from over-investing. You decide what "good enough" means *before* you're tired and invested.

At the end of the box, note where you got to. If the task needs more, schedule another box deliberately rather than just carrying on.

## Try it now

1. Pick a task you're avoiding or one that tends to sprawl.
2. Decide how much time it deserves. Be a little stingy — 15 or 20 minutes is fine.
3. Set a custom timer for that length and start.
4. When it ends, stop. Write one line: what's done, and what (if anything) needs another box.
