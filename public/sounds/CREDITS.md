# Sound credits

The nature sounds are binaural field recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org),
all released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
Credit isn't required, but it's kind. Each file is a trimmed, re-encoded excerpt
(`scripts/encode-sound.mjs`).
