## How it works

Instead of short sprints, you work in blocks of roughly an hour with a longer, proper break. The longer block gives you time to settle into harder work; the longer break is meant to actually restore energy — a walk, a snack, stretching, looking out a window — not just switching to email.

The precise 52 and 17 aren't special. What matters is the pattern: a focused block long enough for deep work, followed by a break long enough to recover.

## Try it now

1. Choose a task that needs sustained thought.
2. Start the 52/17 timer. Work only on that task.
3. When the break starts, leave your desk. Avoid your phone if you can.
4. Afterwards, notice: did the second block feel easier than usual?
