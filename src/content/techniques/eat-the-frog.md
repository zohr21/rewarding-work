## How it works

Your "frog" is the task that matters most and that you're most likely to put off. The idea is to tackle it before email, messages and small tasks take over your day. Small tasks feel productive but can fill all available time.

Doing the dreaded thing early has two benefits: it gets done while you still have energy and before other demands appear, and the relief of having it behind you can make the rest of the day feel lighter.

If the frog is too big to swallow, break it into smaller steps and eat the first one.

## Try it now

1. Look at your to-do list and ask: which one task, if done today, would make the biggest difference?
2. If it's large, use the breakdown tool below to split it into small concrete steps.
3. Decide when you'll start it — ideally before checking email or messages.
4. Do the first step now, or put it first on tomorrow's plan.
