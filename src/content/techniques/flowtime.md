## How it works

Pomodoro-style timers can cut you off just as you get absorbed. Flowtime flips it: a timer counts **up** from when you start. You keep going as long as you're genuinely focused. When you notice your attention slipping — rereading the same line, reaching for your phone — you stop and note how long you worked.

Then you rest in proportion to the effort. A common rule of thumb is a break of about one-fifth of your work time: 25 minutes of work → a 5-minute break; 50 minutes → 10.

Over a few days, your logged sessions show you how long you can typically focus, which is useful information in itself.

## Try it now

1. Pick one task and start the Flowtime timer. It counts up.
2. Work until you notice your focus fading. Be honest — not until you're exhausted.
3. Press stop. The timer suggests a break of about one-fifth of the time you worked.
4. Take that break, then start again.
