## How it works

Pick one habit. Every day you do it, mark the day. After a few days you have a chain of marks, and you start to want to protect it. The visible record turns an invisible habit into something you can see growing.

Make the daily version small enough that you can do it on a bad day — "write 50 words", not "write 1,000". A small habit done most days beats a big one done occasionally.

Chains will break. That's expected and it doesn't erase what you built. This site shows your **best chain** prominently, so a gap is never the headline.

## Try it now

1. Choose one small daily habit.
2. Set it as your habit in the chain tracker below.
3. If you've done it today, mark today.
4. Come back tomorrow and mark it again.
